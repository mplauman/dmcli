---
name: statblock-flavour
description: Generates stat blocks with dmcli and adds brief, plain-language flavor to inspire DM improvisation.
---

Run `dmcli statblock --output xml <NAME> <CR_OR_LEVEL>` (with any requested `-s`, `-r`, or `-f` flags). Use the XML numbers.

Keep it succinct, brief, and in plain language. Provide inspiration to improvise, not details or purple prose:

- **Look & Feel** (1–2 sentences): Immediate sensory impression (appearance, movement, sound/scent). Present tense, no stats.
- **Features** (1 line each): Visual tell or impact when activated, with DC/damage in parentheses.
- **Tactics** (2 short bullets): Combat behavior and retreat instinct based on the numbers.
